About us

Mission statement

We believe in the breathtaking God of the Bible.

We believe God speaks His voice into the heart of all who read His word yearning to truly know Him.

Therefore we speak out to the world to praise God and give honor to His great and holy written word.

We believe

In the God of the Bible

Our work begins with who God is — remarkable, astonishing and worthy of awe.

He speaks

Through His word

God speaks to every heart that reads Scripture yearning to truly know Him.

We speak

To praise and honor Him

Because we believe, we speak out to the world in praise of God and His written word.

“It is written: ‘I believed; therefore I have spoken.’ Since we have that same spirit of faith, we also believe and therefore speak.” 2 Corinthians 4:13 (NIV)
