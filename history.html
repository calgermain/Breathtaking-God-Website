Our history

The story behind “Breathtaking God”

Breathtaking God, Inc. was founded in the spring of 2011 on a simple but profound call:

“…That you may love the LORD your God, listen to His voice and hold fast to Him.” Deuteronomy 30:20 (NIV)
Founded: Spring 2011 Focus: Helping hearts respond to God’s Word

Why “Breathtaking”?

Breathtaking means to take one’s breath away with astonishment or delight. According to Webster’s Dictionary, the word dates to 1864.

We can think of no better word for the God of the Bible — remarkable, astonishing, and the cause of awe.

Hebrews 4:12 (KJV)
