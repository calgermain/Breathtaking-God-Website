What we do

Wise builders

We are determined to build this ministry on a solid foundation. These are the commitments that guide our work.

  1. We build on a solid foundation.

    “The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, because it had its foundation on the rock.” Matthew 7:25 (NIV)

  2. We trust the cornerstone.

    “So this is what the Sovereign LORD says: ‘See, I lay a stone in Zion, a tested stone, a precious cornerstone for a sure foundation; the one who relies on it will never be stricken with panic.’” Isaiah 28:16 (NIV)

  3. We build with care.

    “For no one can lay any foundation other than the one already laid, which is Jesus Christ.” 1 Corinthians 3:11 (NIV)

  4. We sit under the instruction of the LORD.

    “I instruct you in the way of wisdom and lead you along straight paths.” Proverbs 4:11 (NIV)

  5. We run with perseverance the race marked out for us.
